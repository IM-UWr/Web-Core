Rafał Łyżwa distinguished in the Marian Rejewski Competition

PhD student Rafał Łyżwa received an honourable mention in the competition for the best master's thesis in applied mathematics.

We are pleased to announce that our PhD student Rafał Łyżwa received an honourable mention in the Marian Rejewski Competition for the Rector of Lodz University of Technology’s prize for the best master’s thesis in applied mathematics, for his thesis O reprezentacji w teorii operatorów Dunkla funkcji harmonicznych z warunkiem Carlesona (On the representation of harmonic functions satisfying the Carleson condition in Dunkl theory), supervised by Prof. Jacek Dziubański.
