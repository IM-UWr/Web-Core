Achievement Published 19.10.2020 · For: staff, students

Best diploma thesis competition decided

Winners of the 2020 competition for the best diploma theses in mathematics, co-organised by Santander Bank Polska.

We are pleased to announce that two equal prizes in the competition for the best master’s thesis in mathematics went to:

  • Mikołaj Słupiński for the thesis Hidden Markov Models in ultra-high frequency time series processing, supervised by Dr hab. Paweł Lorek,
  • Maciej Kucharski for the thesis Niezależne od wymiaru szacowania transformat Riesza związanych z oscylatorem harmonicznym (Dimension-free estimates for Riesz transforms related to the harmonic oscillator), supervised by Dr hab. Błażej Wróbel.

The winners of the competition for the best bachelor’s thesis in mathematics were chosen during the final held on 23 October on YouTube.

The first prize went to Alicja Kotyla for the thesis O nieskończonej teorii Galois (On infinite Galois theory), supervised by Prof. Krzysztof Krupiński.

Two equal second prizes were awarded to Nela Tomaszewicz for the thesis Statistical Analysis of Climate Data, supervised by Dr Liudmyla Zaitseva, and to Wojciech Prokopowicz for the thesis Spacery losowe na grafach nieskończonych (Random walks on infinite graphs), supervised by Prof. Dariusz Buraczewski.

Nela Tomaszewicz’s thesis also received the audience award.

The other finalists were:

  • Grzegorz Bilka, O pewnym ideale podzbiorów płaszczyzny (On a certain ideal of subsets of the plane; supervisor: P. Borodulin-Nadzieja),
  • Bartosz Chmiela, Statystyczna analiza częstości występowania kodonów w kodzie genetycznym (Statistical analysis of codon frequencies in the genetic code; supervisor: K. Topolski),
  • Maciej Korpalski, Kombinatoryczna charakteryzacja współczynnika pokryciowego dla ideału zbiorów miary zero (A combinatorial characterisation of the covering number of the null ideal; supervisor: J. Kraszewski),
  • Marta Morawiec, Test normalności i niezależności (A test of normality and independence; supervisor: W. Ejsmont).

Congratulations to all winners, finalists and their supervisors.

The competition was co-organised and the prizes funded by Santander Bank Polska SA.
