A paper in the Annals of Mathematics

Terence Tao’s lecture in Wrocław

On 6 September 2017, Fields Medallist Terence Tao gave an open lecture, The Erdős discrepancy problem, in Wrocław. An estimated audience of more than five hundred people attended.
