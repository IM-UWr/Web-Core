Day colours

At the Institute we do not use the week parity shown in USOS, but day colours: for classes held every other week, one group meets on orange days and the other on blue days. Days without classes are not counted, and a swapped day counts as the day whose timetable it follows.

  • Blue day — the weekday occurs in the semester for an odd time
  • Orange day — the weekday occurs in the semester for an even time
  • WEDDay swap — classes follow another day’s timetable; the abbreviation tells which (e.g. “WED” — Wednesday timetable)
  • No classes (public holiday, break, Rector’s day off)
  • Saturday and Sunday
  • Today

The year at a glance

Winter semester 2026/27

Semester1 Oct 2026 – 23 Feb 2027
Inauguration of the academic year1 Oct 2026
Classes2 Oct 2026 – 5 Feb 2027
Exam session6 Feb 2027 – 16 Feb 2027
Resit session17 Feb 2027 – 23 Feb 2027

Days without classes

  • 11 November 2026 (Wednesday) — Independence Day (statutory public holiday)
  • 12 November 2026 (Thursday) — Rector's day off
  • 13 November 2026 (Friday) — Rector's day off (University Day)
  • 21 December 2026 – 6 January 2027 — Christmas break

Day swaps

On these days classes follow the timetable of a different weekday:

  • 18 December 2026 (Friday) — Wednesday classes, blue day

Summer semester 2026/27

Semester24 Feb 2027 – 30 Sep 2027
Classes24 Feb 2027 – 17 Jun 2027
Exam session18 Jun 2027 – 1 Jul 2027
Resit session1 Sep 2027 – 9 Sep 2027
Semester break (holidays)2 Jul 2027 – 31 Aug 2027 and 10 Sep 2027 – 30 Sep 2027

Days without classes

  • 26–30 March 2027 (Friday–Tuesday) — Easter break
  • 3 May 2027 (Monday) — Constitution Day (statutory public holiday)
  • 7 May 2027 (Friday) — Rector's day off (Juwenalia student festival)
  • 27 May 2027 (Thursday) — Corpus Christi (statutory public holiday)
  • 28 May 2027 (Friday) — Rector's day off

Day swaps

On these days classes follow the timetable of a different weekday:

  • 24 March 2027 (Wednesday) — Friday classes, blue day
  • 1 April 2027 (Thursday) — Monday classes, blue day
  • 5 May 2027 (Wednesday) — Friday classes, blue day

The dates of the 2026/27 academic year come from Rector’s Order No. 121/2026 of 25 June 2026 (in Polish).

There is no separate break between the semesters: the summer semester begins on 24 February 2027, the day after the winter resit session ends.

For students in the final year of studies of a given level, the Dean, in agreement with the student government, may shift the dates of the exam sessions.

Legal basis

  • Order No. 121/2026 of the Rector of the University of Wrocław of 25 June 2026 on the organisation of the academic year 2026/2027 at the University of Wrocław (PDF, Public Information Bulletin)
  • Order No. 121/2026 replaced Order No. 42/2026 of 18 March 2026: among other things, it set the date of Juwenalia and changed the day swaps. If you come across an earlier version of the calendar, follow Order No. 121/2026.
