Study

The Mathematical Institute runs bachelor's and master's studies in mathematics, a bachelor's programme in Artificial Intelligence in Data Science, Individual Studies in Computer Science and Mathematics (ISIM) jointly with the Institute of Computer Science, and the Postgraduate Qualification Programme in Teaching Mathematics. Doctoral students are trained in the Doctoral College of Mathematics of the University of Wrocław Doctoral School.

About our programmes

We teach mathematics the way we practise it. Classes are taught by active researchers, and the programme combines solid foundations with plenty of choice: from the first year you can match the level of your classes to your abilities and, later on, choose the direction of your own development. The studies teach precise thinking, posing and solving problems, and working with mathematical models and data — skills valued in research, business, technology and education.

In mathematics, first-cycle studies take three years and second-cycle studies two. The first semesters cover the foundations — mathematical analysis, linear algebra and an introduction to mathematics — in a standard or an extended version for those who want to work more intensively from the start. Later, students choose a specialisation that prepares them for research, for applications in data analysis, finance and insurance, or for teaching mathematics. First-cycle studies end with a bachelor's thesis and a bachelor's examination. Second-cycle studies offer an even wider choice of courses, and the master's thesis is written under the supervision of a researcher, often close to their own research.

For those who want to combine mathematics with computer science, we offer programmes on the border of both fields: Artificial Intelligence in Data Science and Individual Studies in Computer Science and Mathematics (ISIM), run jointly with the Institute of Computer Science, with an individual study plan and a tutor. Master's graduates can continue in the Doctoral College of Mathematics of the UWr Doctoral School, where they carry out their own research and prepare a dissertation under a supervisor. For teachers who want to teach mathematics, we offer postgraduate qualification studies.

You are not on your own at the start. Every year group has its coordinator, and in the tutoring room older students help with problems and with catching up. Outside classes, student research clubs organise lectures, workshops and schools, and the Institute's research seminars are open to everyone interested. A semester or a year at another university — in Poland or abroad — can be spent through exchange programmes such as Erasmus+ and MOST. This section also has everything you need day to day: the timetable, the academic calendar with day colours, the rules for course registration, deadlines and forms, and information on examinations and the diploma.

The quality of our teaching is confirmed by the distinction awarded to the mathematics programme by the Polish Accreditation Committee. Our graduates work at technology companies, banks and insurers, in schools and at universities — wherever rigorous thinking and the ability to solve unusual problems count.
