Lecture notes and study materials

Free lecture notes, course scripts and problem sets written by Institute staff, grouped by subject (in Polish).

Free lecture notes, course scripts and problem sets written by the staff of the Institute (two of the linear algebra scripts were written as master’s theses). All materials are in Polish and are grouped by subject; for each item we give the author, the format and the course of the current study programme for which it may be useful.

Many of the scripts were written for earlier editions of the courses (hence, for example, the version labels A or B in some titles), so their scope and notation may differ from the current programme. Files marked “author’s page” are maintained by the authors on their personal websites.

Analysis

Mathematical analysis (1st year)

  • Analiza matematyczna (funkcje jednej zmiennej) (in Polish) — Hanna Marcinkowska. PDF, 784 pp., 7.3 MB. A textbook with exercises after every section; for the course Mathematical Analysis 1 (semester 1).
  • Analiza matematyczna 2 (funkcje wielu zmiennych) (in Polish) — Hanna Marcinkowska. PDF, 230 pp. Limits and continuity, differentiation, Taylor’s formula and extrema, implicit functions, double, line and triple integrals — with exercises; for the course Mathematical Analysis 2 (semester 2).
  • Zastosowanie całki krzywoliniowej do obliczania pola obszaru (in Polish) — Hanna Marcinkowska. PDF, 9 pp. Using line integrals to compute the area of a region; a supplement to Mathematical Analysis 2.
  • Analiza matematyczna 1 B (in Polish) — Paweł Głowacki. Author’s page: a script in 10 chapters (PDF) and problem sets; for the course Mathematical Analysis 1.
  • Analiza matematyczna ISIM 1 and Analiza matematyczna ISIM 2 (in Polish) — Ryszard Szwarc. PDF (author’s page). Scripts for mathematical analysis in the ISIM programme.

Measure and integral (2nd year)

  • Miara i całka (in Polish) — Grzegorz Plebanek. PDF (author’s page), version Omega.1 of September 2025. Script for the course Measure and Integral (semester 3; formerly the lecture Real Functions); problem sets for each chapter are on the author’s page.

Functional analysis (3rd year and second-cycle studies)

  • Analiza funkcjonalna (in Polish) — Tadeusz Pytlik. PDF, 225 pp., 2000. Lectures with exercises; hints and solutions at the end of the script; for the course Functional Analysis 1 (semester 5 or second-cycle studies).
  • Analiza funkcjonalna 1 and Analiza funkcjonalna 2 (in Polish) — Ryszard Szwarc. PDF (author’s page). For the courses Functional Analysis 1 and 2.
  • Wielomiany ortogonalne i problem momentów (in Polish) — Ryszard Szwarc. PDF, 87 pp. Notes for a lecture given in the winter semester of 2003: positive definite sequences, Jacobi matrices, zeros of orthogonal polynomials, the moment problem.

Introduction to mathematics, algebra and combinatorics

Introduction to mathematics (1st year)

  • Wstęp do matematyki A (in Polish) — Ludomir Newelski. Web page (author’s page). For the course Introduction to Mathematics (semester 1).
  • Zbiór zadań ze Wstępu do matematyki (in Polish) — Jan Kraszewski. PDF (author’s page). A problem book for the course Introduction to Mathematics (semester 1).

Linear algebra (1st year)

  • Algebra liniowa 1 (in Polish) — Patrycja Piechaczek. PDF, 138 pp., 2007. A script written as a master’s thesis supervised by Jacek Świątkowski; for the course Linear Algebra 1 (semester 1).
  • Algebra liniowa 2 (in Polish) — Barbara Szczepańska. PDF, 243 pp., 2007. A script written as a master’s thesis supervised by Jacek Świątkowski; for the course Linear Algebra 2 (semester 2).
  • Algebra liniowa 2 (in Polish) — Ludomir Newelski. Web page (author’s page). For the course Linear Algebra 2 (semester 2).

Algebra (2nd year)

  • Algebra 1 (skrypt do wykładu A) (in Polish) — Roman Wencel. PDF, 123 pp., 2004. Groups, rings and fields; for the course Algebra 1 (semester 3).

Combinatorics

  • Zbiór zadań z kombinatoryki (in Polish) — Światosław R. Gal. PDF (author’s page). A problem book, useful for the courses Introduction to Combinatorics (semester 1) and Combinatorics.

Topology

  • Wstęp do topologii (A) (in Polish) — Paweł Krupski. PDF, 113 pp. For the course Topology or Topology R (semester 2).

Differential equations

  • Równania różniczkowe (A1) (in Polish) — Andrzej Raczyński. PDF, 156 pp., 2010. For the course Differential Equations 1 (semester 4).
  • Warsztaty z równań różniczkowych cząstkowych (in Polish) — Piotr Biler, Grzegorz Karch, Tadeusz Nadzieja, Dariusz Wrzosek. PDF, 140 pp., Toruń 2003. Lecture notes from a workshop on partial differential equations: wave equations, the heat equation, the Poisson equation, evolution problems.

Probability and statistics

Probability and simulation

  • Spacery losowe (in Polish) — Dariusz Buraczewski, Piotr Dyszewski. PDF on the author’s page (section “Skrypty do zajęć”). Random walks: a script prepared for a workshop organised by the Polish Children’s Fund (Krajowy Fundusz na rzecz Dzieci) in 2015.
  • Spacery losowe na skończonych grafach (in Polish) — Dariusz Buraczewski. PDF on the author’s page (section “Skrypty do zajęć”). Random walks on finite graphs: a script for a lecture given in 2014/15.
  • Symulacje stochastyczne i teoria Monte Carlo (in Polish) — Tomasz Rolski. PDF, 284 pp., version 2.5 of February 2018 (work in progress). For the course Introduction to Simulation and Monte Carlo Methods (semester 5 or second-cycle studies).

Statistics and data analysis

  • Elementarna analiza danych (in Polish) — Andrzej Dąbrowski. Author’s page: lectures and exercises in R (Rmd files), a book on exploratory data analysis (PDF, epub, bookdown) and problem sets.
  • Analiza danych jakościowych (in Polish) — Andrzej Dąbrowski. Author’s page: lecture notes (2017, HTML) and problem sets on qualitative data analysis.

Actuarial mathematics

  • Matematyka ubezpieczeń majątkowych i osobowych (in Polish) — Ryszard Szekli. PDF (author’s page). For the course of the same name (Property and Personal Insurance Mathematics; second-cycle studies, actuarial and financial mathematics specialisation).

Mathematics teaching

Materials by Danuta Zaremba for students preparing to teach mathematics at school.

  • Repetytorium z matematyki elementarnej (in Polish) — Danuta Zaremba. PDF, 34 pp. A review of elementary mathematics: percentages, equations and inequalities with absolute value and with a parameter; with answers to the exercises.
  • Wybrane zagadnienia z geometrii płaszczyzny (in Polish) — Danuta Zaremba. PDF, 60 pp. Basic facts of Euclidean plane geometry with exercises; hints or solutions are given for some of them.
  • Kilka rad dla studentów odbywających praktykę nauczycielską w szkole podstawowej i gimnazjum (in Polish) — Danuta Zaremba. PDF, 17 pp. Advice on preparing and teaching lessons during the teaching placement; for the placement rules see Teaching placements.

LaTeX and computer graphics

  • Wprowadzenie do systemu LaTeX (in Polish) — Karol Selwat. PDF, 42 pp. An introduction to LaTeX written for students of the Institute; useful for the course LaTeX (semesters 2–6).
  • Pracownia graficzna wizualizacji obiektów matematycznych (in Polish) — Dariusz Buraczewski, Tomasz Elsner. PDF on the author’s page (section “Skrypty do zajęć”). A script for the class on visualising mathematical objects, 2011/12.
