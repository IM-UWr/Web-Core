Archived study programmes in mathematics

Earlier study programmes in mathematics, with learning outcomes and resolutions of the Faculty Council of Mathematics and Computer Science.

Earlier study programmes in mathematics at the Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science of the University of Wrocław. All documents are in Polish.

Programmes in force from 1 October 2019

Study programmes in mathematics in force from 1 October 2019 (under the resolution of the University Senate of 25 September 2019):

  • First-cycle (bachelor’s) programme
  • Second-cycle (master’s) programme
  • Resolution of the Faculty Council of 24 September 2019 on the minimum requirements for completing a semester in mathematics
  • Resolution of the Faculty Council of 24 September 2019 on the requirements for re-enrolment in a semester in mathematics

Programmes in force from 1 October 2017

  • First-cycle (bachelor’s) and second-cycle (master’s) programmes
  • Learning outcomes for first-cycle studies
  • Learning outcomes for second-cycle studies
  • Resolution of the Faculty Council of 27 September 2016 on the conditions for permission to re-enrol in a semester of first-cycle studies in mathematics

The individual resolutions of the Faculty Council that make up the consolidated text above were adopted on 23 May 2017 and 25 September 2018.

Programmes in force from 1 October 2016

  • First-cycle (bachelor’s) and second-cycle (master’s) programmes
  • Learning outcomes for first- and second-cycle studies
  • Resolution of the Faculty Council of 27 September 2016 on the conditions for permission to re-enrol in a semester of first-cycle studies in mathematics

The individual resolutions of the Faculty Council that make up the consolidated text above were adopted on 19 January 2016 and 27 September 2016.

Programmes in force from 1 October 2013

Study programmes in force from 1 October 2013 (also binding on students admitted in February 2013), with changes to the second-cycle programme in force from 1 October 2015:

  • First-cycle (bachelor’s) and second-cycle (master’s) programmes
  • Learning outcomes for first- and second-cycle studies
  • Previous version of the second-cycle programme, in force from 1 October 2013

An interpretation of some provisions of the programme can be found here.

The individual resolutions of the Faculty Council that make up the consolidated text above were adopted on:

  • 21 May 2013
  • 24 September 2013
  • 18 November 2014
  • 22 September 2015
  • 27 September 2016

Earlier programmes

  • Study programme in mathematics (PDF) — in force from 1 October 2012 (until the winter semester of the academic year 2012/2013).
  • Study programme in mathematics (PDF) — in force from 1 October 2011. Various editorial errors in this programme were corrected on 1 May 2012 and 15 June 2012.

The individual resolutions of the Faculty Council that make up the consolidated text of the programme were adopted on:

  • 19 May and 16 June 2009
  • 27 April 2010
  • 19 October 2010
  • 14 June 2011
  • 27 September 2011
  • 22 January 2013
  • 24 September 2013
  • 18 November 2014

The old study programme applies to students of the long-cycle (uniform master’s) programme.
