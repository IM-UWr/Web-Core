Exams and diploma

Past diploma exam papers

Archive of written bachelor's and master's exam papers in mathematics (2002–2026).

On this page
  1. Bachelor’s exam
  2. Master’s exam

Problems from the written diploma exams in mathematics, 2002–2026: the bachelor’s exam (tests, answers, open problems) and the written part of the master’s exam. Exam rules, syllabi and dates are on the Exams and diploma page. The exam papers are in Polish.

Past papers show the type and level of the problems. The current syllabus is set by the documents of the Diploma Exams Committee — the bachelor’s exam syllabus and the master’s exam syllabi.

Bachelor’s exam

For each sitting you will find the test, the same test with answers and the open problems; for a few sittings also worked solutions. In years with two February sittings they are marked as the 1st and 2nd sitting. A dash (—) means the file is not available.
