Course syllabi (2025/26)

Syllabi of courses offered by the Doctoral College of Mathematics in the academic year 2025/26 (in Polish).

Summer semester — lectures: Functional analysis 2 · Econometrics 2 · Fourier methods in ergodic theory · Statistical learning

Summer semester — seminars: Seminar of the Bioinformatics and Multiomics Analysis Team at the Clinical Research Centre · Noncommutative C*-algebras

Winter semester — lectures: Probabilistic foundations of AI · Riemann surfaces · Introduction to real harmonic analysis and singular integrals · Algebraic number theory · Topological groups · Survival analysis · Algebraic geometry and model theory · Dynamical systems

Winter semester — seminars: High-dimensional probability
