Department of Set Theory and Topology

Set theory, general topology and their applications in measure theory and Banach spaces.

set theorytopologyBanach spacesmeasure theoryBoolean algebras

About

The department works in set theory and general topology and their applications in analysis: measure theory, Banach spaces of continuous functions, Boolean algebras and ideals.

For many years it has co-organised the Winter School in Abstract Analysis in Hejnice (Czech Republic).
