Department of Mathematical Analysis

Orthogonal polynomials, noncommutative probability and discrete harmonic analysis.

orthogonal polynomialsnoncommutative probabilityoperatorscombinatorics

About

The department works on orthogonal polynomials and the moment problem, operator theory, discrete harmonic analysis, and noncommutative (quantum) probability and its connections with combinatorics.

It runs the seminar “Discrete Harmonic Analysis and Noncommutative Probability” and co-organises the recurring workshops on noncommutative probability in Będlewo.
