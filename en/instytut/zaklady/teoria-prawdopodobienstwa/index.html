Department of Probability Theory

Stochastic processes and applied probability, including insurance and finance.

stochastic processesGaussian processesrisk theoryqueueing theoryMarkov chains

About

The department continues the Wrocław tradition in probability theory. Research covers stochastic processes (including Gaussian and Lévy processes), risk theory and insurance mathematics, queueing theory, Markov chains and stochastic modelling.

It runs the seminar “Probability Theory and Stochastic Modelling” and organises the recurring Stochastic Models conference in Będlewo.
