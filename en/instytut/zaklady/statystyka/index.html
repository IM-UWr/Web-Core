Department of Statistics

Mathematical statistics, methods for high-dimensional data and their applications.

high-dimensional statisticsvariable selectionstatistical testingapplications in biology and medicine

About

The department works in mathematical statistics and its applications: methods for high-dimensional data (including SLOPE and FDR control), statistical testing and Bayesian statistics, and the analysis of biological and medical data, including proteomics.

It runs the “Statistics” seminar and coordinates the data analysis specialisation.
