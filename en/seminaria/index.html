Research seminars of the Mathematical Institute. Everyone is welcome.

Research seminars are a regular part of academic life at the Institute: this is where new results and work in progress are presented and discussed. Each research department runs its own seminar, which meets regularly during the academic year. Talks are given by members of staff and doctoral students of the Institute, as well as by visitors from other institutions in Poland and abroad, in Polish or in English.

The date and room are given with each talk, as they may vary. Each seminar's page lists its coordinators, upcoming talks with abstracts and an archive of past meetings; all talks also appear in the Institute calendar. We warmly invite students — a seminar is a good way to find out what a department works on — as well as guests from outside the University. Master's seminars, which form part of the degree programme, are described in the Study section.
