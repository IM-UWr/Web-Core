Journals edited at the Institute

Applicationes Mathematicae

A journal dedicated to applied mathematics, especially mathematical modelling, partial differential equations and their applications; it also publishes papers on mathematics of finance and insurance, optimization, probability and statistics, and numerical methods. Formerly Zastosowania Matematyki.

Publisher
Institute of Mathematics, Polish Academy of Sciences
ISSN
1233-7234 (print) · 1730-6280 (online)
Journal website

Colloquium Mathematicum

Original papers of moderate length addressed to a broad mathematical audience: research results, new proofs of important theorems and research-expository papers in all fields of pure mathematics. The journal was founded by Bronisław Knaster, Edward Marczewski, Hugo Steinhaus and Władysław Ślebodziński.

Publisher
Institute of Mathematics, Polish Academy of Sciences
Editorial office
Institute of Mathematics, University of Wrocław, pl. Grunwaldzki 2/4, 50-384 Wrocław, Poland
ISSN
0010-1354 (print) · 1730-6302 (online)
Journal website

Probability and Mathematical Statistics

Original contributions to the theory of probability and mathematical statistics. The journal was founded in 1980 on the initiative of the Wrocław probability community led by Kazimierz Urbanik and Czesław Ryll-Nardzewski and the statistics community represented by Witold Klonecki; since 2007 it has been an affiliated journal of the Institute of Mathematical Statistics. All papers are freely available (diamond open access).
