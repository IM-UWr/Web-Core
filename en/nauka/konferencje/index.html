Conferences

Conferences and workshops organised or co-organised by members of the Mathematical Institute.

The Institute's research departments organise and co-organise conferences, workshops and schools that bring together mathematicians from Poland and abroad. Their topics reflect the research carried out at the Institute, from pure mathematics to its applications. The meetings take place in Wrocław and at conference centres elsewhere in Poland and abroad, and some of them are recurring events held in successive editions.

Upcoming conferences are listed at the top and past ones below, arranged by year. Each entry gives the dates and venue and links to the conference website, where the organisers publish the programme, registration details and practical information. Upcoming conferences also appear in the Institute calendar. We warmly invite researchers, doctoral students and students to take part.
