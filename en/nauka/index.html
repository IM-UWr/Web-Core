Research

The Mathematical Institute carries out research in eight departments — from algebra and model theory, through analysis, geometry, topology and differential equations, to probability theory and statistics. In the evaluation of research activity for 2017–2021, the discipline of mathematics at the University of Wrocław received the highest category, A+.
