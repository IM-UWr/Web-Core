Research

The Mathematical Institute carries out research in eight departments — from algebra and model theory, through analysis, geometry, topology and differential equations, to probability theory and statistics. In the evaluation of research activity for 2022–2025, the discipline of mathematics at the University of Wrocław once again received the highest category, A+.

Research at the Institute

Three departments work where logic, algebra, topology and geometry meet. The Department of Algebra and Model Theory works in model theory — a branch of mathematical logic — and its connections with algebra and number theory, including definable groups and fields with operators. The Department of Set Theory and Topology links set theory and topology with measure theory and the theory of Banach spaces, while the Department of Geometry studies groups through the spaces they act on, including non-positively curved complexes.

Analysis has a strong tradition in Wrocław. The Department of Functional Analysis continues the school of harmonic analysis founded by Andrzej Hulanicki, while the Department of Mathematical Analysis works on orthogonal polynomials, discrete harmonic analysis and noncommutative probability. The Department of Differential Equations studies nonlinear partial differential equations, including models of chemotaxis and pattern formation. The Department of Probability Theory focuses on stochastic processes, risk theory and queueing theory, the Department of Statistics on methods for high-dimensional data and their applications in biology and medicine, and the Mathematics Education and Outreach Unit on mathematics education.

Day-to-day research life centres on seminars: each department runs its own, with talks by staff, doctoral students and visitors from Poland and abroad. Research is funded mainly through grants from the National Science Centre — from PRELUDIUM for doctoral students to OPUS and SONATA BIS — and the Institute has also taken part in European programmes, including the Marie Curie network MODNET in model theory.

Members of the Institute organise and co-organise recurring meetings such as the Forum on Partial Differential Equations and the Stochastic Models conference in Będlewo, and the Winter School in Abstract Analysis in Hejnice, Czech Republic. In 2025 our faculty, together with Wrocław University of Science and Technology, hosted the 44th Conference on Stochastic Processes and their Applications. Doctoral and habilitation degrees are conferred by the Mathematics Discipline Council, and public PhD defences are announced on the “PhD defences” page.

Wrocław mathematics also has its own journals. The editorial office of Colloquium Mathematicum, published since 1947, is based at the Institute, and the Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science, together with Wrocław University of Science and Technology, supports Probability and Mathematical Statistics, a journal founded in 1980 on the initiative of Wrocław probabilists and statisticians.

Recent highlights include the paper by Ben Krause, Mariusz Mirek and Terence Tao in the Annals of Mathematics (2022), which received the ICCM Best Paper Award, and the invited lecture Prof. Mirek gave at the International Congress of Mathematicians in Philadelphia in 2026. In the same year Prof. Tadeusz Januszkiewicz received the Wacław Sierpiński Medal and Dr Maciej Kucharski the Kazimierz Kuratowski Prize, while in 2024 Prof. Małgorzata Bogdan was named a Fellow of the Institute of Mathematical Statistics. The experts who assessed Wrocław mathematics in the 2022–2025 evaluation highlighted the quality of its publications and the breadth and strength of its research.
