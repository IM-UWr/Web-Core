Mathematics outreach

Together with the Wrocław Mathematicians Foundation, the Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science and the Wrocław Branch of the Polish Mathematical Society, mathematicians from the Institute run maths circles and camps for pupils, competitions, popular science lectures and training for teachers. Below are the main initiatives, with links to their websites.
