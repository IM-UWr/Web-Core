Mathematics outreach

Together with the Wrocław Mathematicians Foundation, the Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science and the Wrocław Branch of the Polish Mathematical Society, mathematicians from the Institute run maths circles and camps for pupils, competitions, popular science lectures and training for teachers. Below are the main initiatives, with links to their websites.

For pupils

Maths circles, competitions, olympiads and camps — from primary to secondary school.

Inter-school Maths Circles (external website)

Classes for mathematically talented pupils, run by the Wrocław Mathematicians Foundation in cooperation with the Mathematical Institute. The olympiad circles prepare for the Mathematical Olympiad and the Junior Mathematical Olympiad; there are also “Mathematics for Thinkers” groups. Participants can take part in the Foundation's competitions, city games and camps.

For whompupils from year 3 of primary school to secondary school

Groups, schedule and enrolment fmw.math.uni.wroc.pl

Wrocław Mathematical Marathon (external website)

An open, free individual competition with the final held at the Mathematical Institute. Contestants compete in categories for primary school years 3–5 and 6–8, secondary school pupils, and adults — professionals and amateurs. Pupils are entered by their schools and maths circles.

For whompupils and adult maths enthusiasts

Rules and registration fmw.math.uni.wroc.pl

Mathematical Linguistics Olympiad (external website)

An interdisciplinary competition in which pupils solve problems about unfamiliar languages, uncovering their patterns and structures by deductive reasoning. It serves as the national qualifying round for the International Linguistics Olympiad and is organised by the Wrocław Mathematicians Foundation.

For whomsecondary school pupils

Olympiad website olm.math.uni.wroc.pl

Young Discoverers (external website)

A project promoting mathematics, computer science and artificial intelligence, run in 2025–2026 by the Wrocław Mathematicians Foundation and the Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science. It includes the Lower Silesian Young Discoverers Congresses — two-day events with lectures, workshops and an outdoor game — as well as one-day workshops and the University Mathematical Olympiad Camp.

For whomsecondary school pupils

Project website odkrywcy.math.uni.wroc.pl

Maths camps (external website)

The Wrocław Mathematicians Foundation organises camps for mathematically gifted young people, including the Winter Schools of Mathematics (held since 1989, about 100 participants) and camps preparing for the Mathematical Olympiad and the Junior Mathematical Olympiad. Classes at the Winter Schools are taught by students, PhD students and researchers of the Institute; pupils are nominated by their mathematics teachers or maths circle leaders.

For whommathematically gifted young people

Winter Schools of Mathematics fmw.math.uni.wroc.pl

University classes (external website)

Mathematics and computer science classes under the patronage of the Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science, at Primary Schools No. 3 and No. 76 in Wrocław. Pupils follow a dedicated mathematics curriculum with extra teaching hours; admission is based on a mathematical aptitude test.

For whomcandidates for year 7 of primary school

Admissions and information wmi.uwr.edu.pl

Artificial Intelligence Olympiad (external website)

A three-stage national competition; the Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science is one of its main organisers, and the second stage is held in several cities, including Wrocław. The olympiad website offers problems from previous editions and training lectures.

For whomschool pupils

Olympiad website oai.edu.pl

For teachers

Seminars, conferences and professional development courses for mathematics teachers.

I³ seminar for teachers (external website)

Weekly meetings of mathematics teachers and students at the Mathematical Institute during the academic year, held since the summer semester of 2003/2004. They take place in groups for primary and secondary school teachers, usually with a plenary session once a month. The programme includes teaching workshops, popular science talks, news from the professional literature and discussions with invited guests. The name stands for Inspiration, Individualisation, Interdisciplinarity.

For whommathematics teachers and students

Seminar page fmw.math.uni.wroc.pl

Lower Silesian Congress of Mathematics Teachers (external website)

A one-day conference at the Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science for secondary school mathematics teachers from Lower Silesia and the surrounding area, with lectures and workshops. The first edition took place in March 2025 and the second in March 2026; each was attended by more than 120 teachers.

For whomsecondary school mathematics teachers

Congress website dokonamat2.math.uni.wroc.pl

Courses and training by the Foundation (external website)

The Wrocław Mathematicians Foundation organises or supports courses for teachers: the postgraduate programme “Modern Methods of Teaching Mathematics”, courses on specific difficulties in learning mathematics (dyscalculia) and on programming in years 1–3, professional English and German courses for mathematics teachers, and courses for holiday camp tutors and leaders.

For whomteachers (some courses also for students and parents)

Training offer fmw.math.uni.wroc.pl

For everyone

Popular science lectures, a portal with problems and articles, and archived materials for self-study.

Wrocław Mathematical Portal (external website)

A portal promoting mathematics, running since 2007 as a joint initiative of the Mayor of Wrocław and the Rector of the University of Wrocław; its editor-in-chief is Małgorzata Mikołajczyk of the Institute. It publishes articles and curiosities, a calendar of mathematical events, competition news and materials for teachers, and runs problem-solving leagues — 12 school and thematic leagues.

For whompupils, teachers, parents and maths enthusiasts

Go to the portal matematyka.wroc.pl

Wrocław Mathematical Meetings (external website)

A series of popular science talks held at the Mathematical Institute once a month throughout the school year since 1991. The speakers are researchers and PhD students of the Institute and guests from other universities.

For whomschool pupils, teachers and students

Lecture calendar fmw.math.uni.wroc.pl

Lower Silesian Science Festival (external website)

The festival takes place every September. The Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science prepares popular science lectures in mathematics and computer science for it, and the Wrocław Mathematical Marathon is also held as part of the festival.

For whomeveryone interested, including school groups

Festival programme festiwal.wroc.pl

archive

“Trapez” weekly (external website)

A mathematical weekly written by Jarosław Wróblewski of the Institute, published from 2015 to 2019 (221 issues). It contains puzzles and problems — with solutions in later issues — arranged in thematic series, e.g. on the AM–GM inequality, the Chinese remainder theorem and colouring problems, as well as a series for beginning olympiad participants.

Issue archive math.uni.wroc.pl

archive

Mathematics for Thinkers (external website)

A course run at the Institute in 2008/09 and 2009/10, meant to show that mathematics is interesting, wise and useful, and that learning it is not about reproducing memorised algorithms. The course materials — ten parts (with and without solutions) and self-assessment tests — are available for independent study.

Course materials math.uni.wroc.pl

Organisers

The Institute's unit, the foundation and the learned society that run and support these initiatives.

Mathematics Education and Outreach Unit

The Institute's unit working on mathematics education and the training of future teachers, as well as on popularising mathematics among pupils and teachers.

Unit page

Wrocław Mathematicians Foundation (external website)

Established in 2007, the foundation continues the work of the Wrocław Mathematicians Scholarship Foundation (1991–2007). It awards scholarships to talented pupils, students and young mathematicians, runs inter-school maths circles, organises competitions, camps and popular science events, trains teachers, and supports the publication of books, teaching materials and websites about mathematics.

For whompupils, teachers and students

Foundation website fmw.math.uni.wroc.pl

Polish Mathematical Society, Wrocław Branch (external website)

The Wrocław branch of the Polish Mathematical Society, open to people with a background in mathematics or a related field. It runs the Competition for the Best Student Paper in Probability and Applied Mathematics — one of the oldest still-running student paper competitions in Poland. The competition dates back to 1967, when it was initiated by Józef Łukaszewicz of the University of Wrocław.

For whomstudents and mathematicians

Branch website ptm.math.uni.wroc.pl
